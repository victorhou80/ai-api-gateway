# 14. Ecommerce Value-Added Services

## Core Decision

Ecommerce customers should not be retained only through AI usage or copywriting task cards.

The stronger business is:

> AI usage + content production + ad testing guidance + search/SEO optimization + monthly review.

This creates three benefits:

- more revenue lines beyond low-margin token usage
- stronger retention because the service touches daily operations
- easier upsell from 39/99/199 task cards into monthly packages

## Sales Logic

Do not say:

```text
我们帮你投流赚钱。
我们保证 ROI。
我们保证搜索排名。
我们保证爆单。
```

Say:

```text
我们帮你把素材、关键词、投放测试和复盘流程标准化。
效果受产品、价格、页面、评价、预算、人群和平台竞争影响，我们不承诺爆单，但会把每一步变得可测试、可复盘、可迭代。
```

## Three Upsell Paths

### Path A: From Copy To Material System

Trigger:

- customer buys 39/99/199 task card
- customer says content is useful
- customer has more SKUs or recurring content demand

Message:

```text
这次你买的是单次文案。
如果你们每周都要上新或发短视频，不建议每次零散买。
可以升级月度素材陪跑：每月给你做选题、脚本、商品文案更新和一次复盘。
这样你们会有一个持续素材库，而不是临时要用才写。
```

### Path B: From Material To Ad Testing

Trigger:

- customer asks how to投流
- customer already has素材 but no testing plan
- customer has ad spend but cannot read results

Message:

```text
投流前不要先加预算，先把素材测试计划做出来。
我们可以做投流素材测试陪跑：帮你拆卖点、分素材角度、设计测试顺序、看日报数据。
我们不承诺 ROI，但能帮你减少盲测和乱烧预算。
```

### Path C: From Content To SEO/Search

Trigger:

- customer says自然流量弱
- product title is messy
- content does not cover search keywords

Message:

```text
你现在的问题不只是文案，而是搜索词和内容结构没有统一。
可以先做搜索标题体检，再做 SEO 搜索优化月卡。
我们会整理关键词、优化 SKU 标题结构、规划内容关键词，让商品页和内容更容易被目标用户搜到。
不承诺排名，但会把基础搜索信息补齐。
```


```text

不承诺排名，但能把搜索基础补齐，让后面的内容和投流更容易形成闭环。
```

```text

你现在更需要一个组合：
1. 商品文案先跑通；
2. 素材角度持续生产；

4. 投流前做测试计划；

这样才不是买一次内容，而是把店铺的内容和增长流程搭起来。
```

## Compliance Rules

All add-on services must follow these rules:

- do not promise traffic, ranking, conversion, ROI, GMV, or approval
- do not write fake reviews
- do not support fake orders
- do not make false product claims
- do not perform click/search manipulation
- do not take over ad accounts without written authorization
- do not spend customer ad budget without written approval
- do not use "official", "semi-official", "guaranteed", "no ban", or "must explode"
- customer must confirm product facts, qualifications, prices, promotions, and risk claims
