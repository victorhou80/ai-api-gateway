## Risk Flags

Reject or escalate if the customer requests:

- shared account or shared API key
- unlimited usage
- bypassing region/payment/KYC/platform restrictions
- fake reviews, spam, scraping, fraud, gambling, pornography, impersonation
- medical/legal/financial advice as final decision
- exam cheating or academic misconduct
- deepfake, voice cloning, or identity imitation
- sensitive personal information processing
- state secrets, important data, or unauthorized trade secrets
