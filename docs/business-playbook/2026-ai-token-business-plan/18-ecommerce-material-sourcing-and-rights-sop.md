# 18. Ecommerce Material Sourcing And Rights SOP

## Core Decision

Material sourcing is not "find something online and use it."

For ecommerce, materials must be collected, licensed, labeled, and archived. Otherwise, the business can lose more money from copyright claims, platform review failures, brand complaints, and customer disputes than it earns from the order.

Use this principle:

> 客户自有素材优先，授权素材第二，商用素材库第三，竞品素材只参考不复制，AI 生成素材必须标记和复核。

## Material Source Hierarchy

| Priority | Source | Can Use Directly? | Risk | Best For |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | customer-owned product photos/videos | yes, after customer confirms ownership | low | product page, ads, short video, listing |
| 2 | customer supplier/manufacturer assets | yes, only with customer/supplier authorization | medium | product specs, product photos, factory material |
| 3 | customer-shot phone videos/photos | yes | low | short video, real use scenes, before/after workflow |
| 4 | authorized UGC/KOL material | yes, only within contract scope | medium | social proof, lifestyle videos |
| 5 | commercial stock libraries | yes, under license terms | medium | background, lifestyle, generic scenes |
| 6 | AI-generated images/video | yes, after tool/license/platform review | medium | concept visuals, backgrounds, scene mockups |
| 7 | competitor pages/ads/videos | no direct use | high | reference only: angle, structure, claims to avoid |
| 8 | random web images/social media screenshots | no | high | do not use without permission |

## Six Practical Sources

### 1. Customer-Owned Materials

Ask the customer for:

- product photos
- product videos
- product link
- SKU table
- product manual
- packaging copy
- brand guide
- store policies
- FAQ/customer-service scripts
- past ads
- past livestream scripts
- product certificates or test reports

This is the safest and most useful material source.

Sales message:

```text
素材最好先用你们自己的：商品图、视频、详情页、说明书、客服问题、过去投放素材。
这些最贴近产品，也最不容易有版权和平台审核问题。
```

### 2. Supplier / Manufacturer Materials

For ecommerce, many sellers get photos and specs from suppliers. These can be useful, but authorization must be clear.

Ask:

- did the supplier allow commercial use?
- can the customer modify the images?
- can the customer use them for ads?
- can the customer use them outside the original marketplace?
- are there logos, patents, certifications, or third-party brands in the material?

Customer confirmation:

```text
请确认这些供应商图片/视频/说明书，你们有权用于店铺、广告和二次编辑。
如果只能用于某个平台，或不能投广告，需要提前说明。
```

### 3. Self-Shot Material

For small stores, recommend simple phone shooting.

Shot list:

- product front/back/side
- product in hand
- packaging
- size comparison
- usage scene
- detail close-up
- problem-solution comparison
- 5-10 second real use clip
- customer-service issue demonstration

Minimum shooting guide:

```text
用手机横竖各拍一版。
自然光，干净背景。
每个商品拍 5 张图 + 3 段 5-10 秒视频。
不要加滤镜，不要加夸张字幕，先保留原始素材。
```

## Commercial Stock Libraries

Stock libraries are useful for background scenes, generic lifestyle images, and poster support, but they should not replace real product photos.

Common options:

| Library | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pexels | free photos/videos | check license, avoid implying endorsement |
| Pixabay | free photos/videos/audio | check trademark/people restrictions |
| Unsplash | free images | avoid selling unmodified images or using as competing database |
| Canva | templates, icons, design assets | check Free/Pro/Branded/AI content license rules |
| Adobe Stock / Shutterstock / iStock | paid stock | keep receipts and license records |
| Envato Elements | design/video/audio templates | check project/use terms |

License notes from official pages:

- Pexels says photos/videos can be used for free and modified, but identifiable people cannot be shown offensively, and you cannot imply endorsement or use content as a trademark.
- Pixabay's license summary allows free use and modification subject to prohibited uses, but warns about recognizable trademarks, brands, people, and misleading use.
- Unsplash allows free commercial and non-commercial image use, but not selling images without significant modification or compiling them into a competing service.
- Canva's content license depends on the content category, such as Free, Pro, Branded, and AI-generated content. Check the license for each exported design.

Sources:

- Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/
- Pixabay license summary: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
- Unsplash license: https://unsplash.com/license
- Canva content license: https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/

## Competitor Materials

Competitor material can be used only for research and reference.

Allowed:

- analyze title structure
- extract common keyword categories
- compare selling-point angles
- summarize public review themes
- identify content formats
- study ad hook patterns

Not allowed:

- copy competitor images
- copy competitor videos
- copy product page text
- copy review screenshots
- remove watermarks
- imitate brand assets
- use competitor customer photos
- imply affiliation

Internal rule:

```text
竞品只做结构分析，不做素材搬运。
```

## UGC / KOL Materials

If a customer wants to use influencer or buyer-generated content, require authorization.

Confirm:

- who owns the video/photo
- where it can be used
- whether ads are allowed
- allowed platforms
- usage period
- whether editing is allowed
- whether subtitles/cropping are allowed
- whether the creator's face/name/voice can be used

Minimum authorization wording:

```text
授权方同意客户及服务商在【平台】用于【店铺内容/广告投放/详情页/短视频】，
使用期限为【期限】，允许【剪辑/字幕/裁切/二次编辑】，授权范围不含【除外范围】。
```

## AI-Generated Materials

AI-generated material can help with:

- background scene ideas
- poster concepts
- lifestyle mockups
- storyboard drafts
- product-scene visualization
- ad concept variations

But do not use AI generation to:

- create fake reviews
- fabricate before/after evidence
- impersonate real users
- fake celebrity/KOL endorsement
- create misleading product effects
- copy a competitor's image style too closely
- generate trademarked characters or logos

Customer-facing line:

```text
AI 生成图可以做概念和辅助素材，但商品本体、功效、规格、使用效果必须以真实产品资料为准。
```

## Material Collection Workflow

### Step 1: Send Material Request

```text
为了做得准确，也避免版权和平台审核风险，请先发这些资料：
1. 商品链接；
2. 商品原图/视频；
3. 当前标题和卖点；
4. 说明书或参数表；
5. 客服高频问题；
6. 禁用词和不能承诺的效果；
7. 这些图片/视频是否你们有权商用和投放。
```

### Step 2: Classify Materials

Use tags:

```text
客户自有
供应商授权
自自拍摄
商用素材库
UGC 授权
AI 生成
竞品参考
禁止使用
```

### Step 3: Rights Check

Ask:

- who owns it?
- where did it come from?
- can it be used commercially?
- can it be used in ads?
- can it be edited?
- can it be used on all target platforms?
- does it contain people, logos, trademarks, music, or third-party products?
- does it need attribution?

### Step 4: Store Proof

Keep:

- customer confirmation
- supplier authorization
- license URL
- receipt/order number for paid stock
- creator authorization
- screenshot of license terms if needed
- date collected
- intended use

### Step 5: Approve For Production

Only approved materials enter:

- product copy
- poster design
- short-video script
- ad creative
- SEO content
- listing localization
- API workflow training/examples

## Material File Naming

Use a simple naming convention:

```text
customer_platform_sku_source_rights_date_version
```

Example:

```text
ABCShop_TikTokShop_WaterBottle_customerowned_commercialads_2026-06-13_v1.jpg
ABCShop_Amazon_Headphones_supplierauthorized_storeonly_2026-06-13_v1.mp4
```

## Red Lines

Reject or escalate if:

- customer says "just copy this competitor video"
- customer wants to use buyer photos without permission
- customer wants fake before/after
- material contains third-party brand logos without rights
- material contains identifiable people in sensitive context
- customer cannot confirm usage rights
- music/audio license is unclear
- customer wants to remove watermark
- customer wants fake reviews or fake screenshots

## Low-Cost Material Plan For Small Stores

If the customer has no materials, give them this plan:

```text
今天先不用找素材库。
你用手机拍：
1. 商品正面、背面、侧面各 1 张；
2. 包装 1 张；
3. 细节特写 3 张；
4. 手拿或使用场景 3 张；
5. 5-10 秒使用视频 3 段；
6. 你现在的商品链接和标题。

这些就足够先做 39/99/199 的平价任务卡。
```

## Material Sources By Use Case

| Use Case | Best Source |
|---|---|
| product title/selling points | customer product page, manual, specs |
| main image copy | customer product photo, self-shot image |
| short-video script | customer product facts, reviews, FAQ, self-shot clips |
| livestream script | product facts, promotion rules, customer objections |
| SEO/search keywords | public platform search suggestions, customer product category, competitor structure |
| ad angles | customer product benefits, past ads, stock background only if licensed |
| customer-service replies | store policy, FAQ, anonymized customer questions |
| cross-border listing | product specs, target country/language, platform listing rules |

## Customer Disclaimer

Use this in order forms:

```text
客户确认其提供的图片、视频、文字、商标、音乐、KOL/UGC 素材拥有合法使用权，
并授权服务商在本次服务范围内用于文案、脚本、设计、广告素材建议和店铺内容制作。
客户需对产品功效、资质、价格、活动规则和平台合规性进行最终确认。
```

## Final Rule

If material rights are unclear, do not use it in final deliverables.

Use it only as internal reference, or ask the customer to provide authorized/self-shot material.
