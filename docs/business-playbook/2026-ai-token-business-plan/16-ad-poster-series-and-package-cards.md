# 16. Ad Poster Series And Package Cards

## Core Decision

High-ticket packages cannot be sold with vague names.

If a package costs RMB 98,000, the customer must immediately understand:

- service period
- included usage budget or usage pool
- included scenarios
- team seats or departments
- reports and review cadence
- support level
- onboarding and workflow setup
- contract and invoice support
- what is excluded
- what triggers renewal or upgrade

The public-facing materials should have two series:

1. Series A: low-friction ecommerce/cross-border posters for acquisition.
2. Series B: enterprise procurement configuration posters for closing.

## Series A: Ecommerce And Cross-Border Acquisition Posters

Purpose:

- attract leads from Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeCom, private domain, and sales DMs
- make prices easy to understand
- lead customers into the right ladder

Tone:

- simple
- scenario-first
- no technical jargon
- no raw token resale language
- no guaranteed GMV/ROI/ranking

## Series B: Enterprise Procurement Configuration Posters

Purpose:

- explain high-ticket packages clearly
- support boss, finance, procurement, and technical review
- make contract and invoice capability visible
- prevent customers from comparing only raw token price

Tone:

- formal
- precise
- procurement-ready
- service-boundary clear

### Poster B1: RMB 9,800 Trial / Light Managed Package

Title:

```text

```


## Design Notes For Actual Posters

Use consistent sections:

```text
标题
适合谁
价格
包含什么
不包含什么
合同/发票
下一步
```

Do not include:

- "semi-official"
- "internal channel"
- "lowest price"
- "unlimited"
- "no ban"
- "guaranteed ROI"
- "guaranteed ranking"
- "guaranteed GMV"
- "shared API key"

Use visual tags:

```text
可开普票
可开专票
支持合同
用量报表
预算预警
BYOK 可选
不卖共享 key
不承诺 GMV
```

## Final Rule

Every poster must answer this question:

> 客户付这笔钱，具体买到了什么？

If the answer is not visible on the poster, the poster is not ready for sales.
