## 4. Lead Handling SLA

| Lead Type | Response Deadline | Owner |
|---|---:|---|
| A-grade form lead | 10 minutes | SDR then sales |
| A-grade private message | 10 minutes | SDR |
| live high-intent lead | during live or within 15 minutes | live assistant |
| B-grade lead | 2 hours | SDR |
| C-grade lead | 24 hours or auto-reply | SDR/content |
| D-risk lead | same day reject | sales lead |

SLA audit:

- less than 90% on-time response: review staffing
- A-grade lead missed over 1 hour: manager review
- no CRM record after conversation: owner review

## 5. CRM Daily Required Fields

Every active lead must have:

- source
- grade
- use case
- team size
- monthly spend range
- invoice need: no/ordinary/special/unknown
- decision maker
- package recommended
- expected amount
- blocker
- next action
- next action time
- owner

No next action means the lead is not managed.

## 7. Manager Audit Scorecard

Score each salesperson daily out of 100.

| Item | Points | Standard |
|---|---:|---|
| response timeliness | 15 | A leads within 10 minutes |
| CRM completeness | 15 | required fields complete |
| diagnosis quality | 15 | notes include pain, budget, invoice, decision |
| quote speed | 10 | quote sent within 2 hours |
| next-step clarity | 15 | every active deal has next step |
| payment push | 10 | high-intent deals pushed daily |
| compliance | 10 | no forbidden claims |
| review quality | 10 | evening review completed |

Interpretation:

- 90-100: strong
- 80-89: acceptable
- 70-79: needs coaching
- below 70: manager intervention

## 9. Content Review Checklist

Before publishing each post/video/live script:

- no "official", "semi-official", "internal channel"
- no "lowest price", "highest-level", "unlimited", "no ban"
- no off-platform payment bait
- no request for API key/password
- no promise of business result
- includes service boundary
- includes compliant CTA
- if invoice mentioned, says ordinary/special invoice according to actual service

Content score:

| Item | Points |
|---|---:|
| clear target customer | 20 |
| strong pain hook | 20 |
| enterprise trust signal | 20 |
| specific CTA | 20 |
| compliance safe | 20 |

Do not publish under 80 points.

## 11. Weekly Decision Rules

Scale if:

- CAC is below 40% of first-month gross profit
- A-grade lead ratio above 15%
- quote-to-payment above 25%
- refund below 3%
- support load manageable

Keep testing if:

- leads are good but close rate low
- scripts need adjustment
- invoice/procurement blockers are common but solvable

Stop if:

- leads are mostly C-grade
- customers only ask for cheap/unlimited/shared access
- CAC exceeds first-month gross profit
- platform content attracts compliance-risk customers
- support cost eats margin

## 12. Daily Script QA

Manager reviews 5 conversations per salesperson daily.

Check:

- Did the salesperson ask use case first?
- Did they ask team/company vs individual?
- Did they ask invoice/contract need?
- Did they avoid raw token price battle?
- Did they recommend the correct package?
- Did they create a next step?
- Did they update CRM?

Bad example:

```text
客户：多少钱？
销售：29,800，便宜点也可以。
```

Good example:

```text
客户：多少钱？
销售：价格要看场景和月用量。你先回我 3 个信息：主要场景、团队人数、是否需要合同/普票/专票。我再判断你适合试跑包、增长包还是团队包。
```

## 13. End-Of-Day Founder Review

Founder asks:

1. Which customers can pay tomorrow?
2. Which quotes have no next step?
3. Which A-grade leads were not contacted within 10 minutes?
4. Which content produced A-grade leads?
5. Which salesperson needs coaching?
6. Which customer has support or compliance risk?
7. Which renewal must be pushed this week?

If the answer is unclear, the CRM is not good enough.

## 14. 7-Day Launch Sprint

### Day 1

- finalize packages
- prepare quote template
- prepare invoice info form
- prepare CRM
- publish 3 posts
- contact 30 warm leads

### Day 2

- run 5 diagnosis calls
- send first 3 quotes
- publish invoice/procurement content
- hold 1 live session

### Day 3

- follow D1 quotes
- push first payments
- publish technical cost content
- review scripts

### Day 4

- produce 2 case-style posts
- run 5 diagnosis calls
- collect invoice info from high-intent leads
- refine quote wording

### Day 5

- payment push day
- founder personally follows all quotes above RMB 29,800
- publish "how to choose package" content

### Day 6

- live Q&A
- produce clips
- organize lost reasons
- update FAQ

### Day 7

- weekly review
- rank lead sources
- rewrite top 3 scripts
- set next-week targets

## 15. 30-Day Sales Cadence

Week 1: prove flow.

- target: 50 valid leads, 10 quotes, 3 paid customers, at least 10 micro trials and 2 low-price enterprise trials sold

Week 2: prove repeatability.

- target: 100 valid leads, 20 quotes, 8 paid customers, micro-to-trial and trial-to-growth upgrade paths tested

Week 3: push packages.

- target: 150 valid leads, 30 quotes, RMB 150,000+ GMV

Week 4: formalize scale.

- target: RMB 150,000-250,000 monthly GMV run-rate, 10 case assets, CRM discipline stable
