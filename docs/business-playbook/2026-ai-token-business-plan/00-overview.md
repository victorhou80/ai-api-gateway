# 企业 AI 用量托管生意落地手册

Date: 2026-06-14

Goal: clarify two different targets by 2026-12-31:

- company/business target: reach about RMB 500,000 company net profit
- personal target: if the operator's commission is 10%-15% of collected revenue, reach about RMB 500,000 personal commission income

Updated cost edge: actual upstream usage cost is about 30% of the customer-facing face-value price. If the selling/reference price is still RMB 1.2 for USD 1 face-value usage, the internal cost is about RMB 0.36 for USD 1 face-value usage, leaving about 70% raw gross margin before payment fees, tax, refunds, support labor, customer acquisition, compliance, and tooling.

Confirmed operating advantage: the business can issue both ordinary VAT invoices and special VAT invoices. This is important for enterprise customers because it supports company procurement, reimbursement, financial approval, and long-term vendor trust.

## Final Decision

The business should not be positioned as:

- cheap token resale
- API key resale
- account sharing
- foreign model recharge
- card-code/credit reselling
- "semi-official" internal channel
- unlimited, no-ban, lowest-price access

The business should be positioned as:

> Enterprise AI usage management service: stable model access, RMB settlement, budget control, usage reports, team permissions, cost optimization, contracts, ordinary/special VAT invoices, and support.

The low-cost token supply should be treated as a production input, not the public product. Do not disclose "3折成本" in customer-facing copy.

## What Changed After The 30% Cost Update

The original plan assumed a thin-margin model. With actual cost at about 30%, the business has much more room to use low-price trials, referral incentives, channel tests, and enterprise trial packages.

But the strategic direction does not change:

- do not sell raw quota publicly
- do not expose shared API keys
- do not promise unlimited usage
- do not compete only on token price
- do use the cost edge to fund acquisition and improve package value

The practical change is:

> Low-price trials can now be used as a controlled acquisition funnel, while RMB 29,800, RMB 98,000, and value-added ecommerce services become the real profit engine.

## Business Model

Primary revenue:

- B2B AI usage management packages
- enterprise workflow implementation
- ecommerce AI content/material services
- cross-border ecommerce model/API routing
- knowledge-base setup
- support and SLA subscriptions
- procurement and cost-optimization consulting

Secondary revenue:

- diagnostics
- templates
- low-price controlled trials
- short workshops
- paid audits

Avoid:

- high-volume low-ticket consumer resale
- postpaid accounts
- large customer balance pools without controls
- API key sharing
- vague "official/internal channel" claims

Use invoice capability as a filter: prioritize companies that need contracts, ordinary invoices, special invoices, procurement compliance, and monthly usage reports. These customers are less price-only and more likely to buy higher-ticket packages.

## Go/No-Go

Before public selling, obtain:

- written upstream authorization
- resale/integration permission
- data-processing terms
- invoice or equivalent cost proof
- allowed geography/customer scope
- brand-use permission
- upstream failover/price-change rules
- platform category confirmation

If written authorization is not available within 15 days, stop the "usage resale" route and switch to:

- BYOK model routing
- AI workflow implementation
- enterprise procurement consulting
- domestic registered-model solution delivery
- customer-owned official account setup
