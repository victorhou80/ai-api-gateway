## Lead Path

Content exposure -> comment/private message -> official lead form or shop diagnostic -> CRM -> 15-minute diagnosis -> quote -> contract -> prepayment -> activation.

Use compliant calls to action:

- "Click the official appointment form."
- "Submit your team size and monthly usage through the enterprise account form."
- "Purchase the diagnostic service in the shop."
- "Private message 'budget' to receive the in-platform form."

Do not use:

- hidden contact codes
- off-platform transaction bait
- personal payment links
- "avoid review" language

## Lead Form

Title:

`领取 AI 用量预算诊断 / 预约 15 分钟测算`

Subtitle:

`不收集 API Key，不要求账号密码，仅根据业务场景估算预算。`

Fields:

| Field | Options |
|---|---|
| role | boss / technical / operations / procurement / education / other |
| team size | 1-5 / 6-20 / 21-100 / 100+ |
| use case | API / content / customer service / knowledge base / education / office / other |
| current monthly AI spend | 0-1000 / 1000-5000 / 5000-20000 / 20000+ |
| purchase timeline | now / 7 days / 30 days / research |
| budget range | under 1000 / 1000-5000 / 5000-20000 / 20000+ |
| contract/invoice need | no / ordinary invoice / special invoice / contract plus invoice |
| contact | platform-approved field |
| privacy consent | required |

## Lead Scoring

| Grade | Criteria | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A | monthly spend 5000+, team 6+, purchase within 30 days | follow up within 10 minutes |
| B | monthly spend 1000-5000 | sell diagnostic or trial |
| C | personal/low budget | send content/templates |
| D | wants shared accounts, unlimited usage, bypassing rules | reject |

Upgrade a lead by one priority level if the customer explicitly needs a contract, ordinary invoice, special invoice, or company reimbursement. That signal usually means B2B procurement intent and higher willingness to pay for a formal service provider.
