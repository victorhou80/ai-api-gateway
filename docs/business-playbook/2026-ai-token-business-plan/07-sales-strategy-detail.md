# 07. Detailed Sales Strategy

## 1. Sales Thesis

The sales motion should not be "we have cheap token supply." That attracts price shoppers, consumer users, gray-market requests, and high-support low-margin customers.

The sales motion should be:

> We help companies turn AI model usage into a formal, budget-controlled, invoiceable, reportable, supported enterprise service.

Your strongest sales advantages are:

- can issue ordinary VAT invoice and special VAT invoice
- can support company payment and formal procurement
- can provide usage reports for finance and management
- can set budget caps and renewal alerts
- can package service, implementation, and support around low-cost usage
- can speak to boss, finance, technical, and operations roles separately

The invoice capability is not just an admin detail. It is a customer filter. A customer who asks about special invoice, contract, budget, reimbursement, or company payment is usually more valuable than a customer who only asks "how cheap per token?"

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile

### A-Level Customers

| Segment | Pain | Why They Buy | Target Package |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI SaaS / AI tool teams | model cost, unstable billing, growth uncertainty | need predictable usage and technical support | Team / Enterprise |
| software outsourcing and agent studios | many client demos and API tests | need flexible usage, invoice, fast setup | Growth / Team |
| ecommerce content teams | scripts, customer service, product copy, batch generation | want cost control and workflow delivery | Growth + workflow |
| training / education companies | many students or internal teams use AI | need quota control, invoice, training support | Team / Enterprise |
| MCN / content agencies | high-volume content and ideation | need stable batch work and reports | Growth / Team |
| B2B sales/customer-service teams | FAQ, scripts, response drafting, lead scoring | need business workflow and supervision | Implementation + SLA |

### B-Level Customers

| Segment | Issue | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| solo developers | low budget but may refer | sell diagnostic / trial only |
| small creators | high support, low ARPU | sell template or workflow pack |
| early-stage founders | uncertain usage | sell RMB 9,800 trial |
| traditional SMEs curious about AI | education cost high | sell workshop first |

### C/D-Level Customers To Avoid

- students looking for cheap access
- users asking for shared accounts
- users asking for unlimited quota
- users asking for "will it be blocked?"
- customers asking to bypass platform/model rules
- customers wanting postpaid from first order
- customers using sensitive personal data without compliance awareness
- customers whose only decision criterion is lowest price

## 3. Persona Strategy

### Boss / Founder

What they care about:

- "Will AI cost get out of control?"
- "Can I give this to my team without chaos?"
- "Can finance reimburse it?"
- "Will someone be responsible if it breaks?"

Sales angle:

> Your problem is not buying cheaper tokens. Your problem is making AI usage controllable, reportable, and usable by a team.

Offer:

- Growth package for first team test
- Team package after usage pattern is clear
- monthly usage report for management

### Technical Lead

What they care about:

- API stability
- usage accounting
- model routing
- budget caps
- logs and troubleshooting
- not exposing keys to employees

Sales angle:

> We help you manage usage, routing, budget, and reporting, so you do not have to hand out keys or manually reconcile bills.

Offer:

- BYOK model routing if they already have official accounts
- managed usage package if they need RMB settlement and invoice
- technical support/SLA if they run production workloads

### Operations / Content Lead

What they care about:

- output speed
- usable workflow
- templates
- team adoption
- cost per batch
- stable process

Sales angle:

> We do not just give quota. We help turn your content/customer-service workflow into a repeatable AI process.

Offer:

- Growth package + content workflow
- AI content factory implementation
- monthly support

### Finance / Procurement

What they care about:

- contract
- invoice type
- payment entity
- invoice title and tax number
- service item
- budget cap
- reconciliation

Sales angle:

> We can provide contract, ordinary/special invoice, service description, payment record, usage report, and renewal warning, which makes AI usage easier to manage internally.

Offer:

- formal B2B contract package
- monthly/quarterly usage statement
- package renewal rather than random recharges

## 4. Offer Ladder

The sales path should move customers upward:

1. Free budget calculator or usage checklist.
2. RMB 399-999 paid diagnostic.
3. RMB 2,980 light enterprise trial.
4. RMB 4,980 standard enterprise trial.
5. RMB 9,800 enterprise trial package.
6. RMB 29,800 growth package.
7. RMB 98,000 team package.
8. RMB 300,000+ enterprise package.
9. Monthly support/SLA renewal.

Do not sell the full service below RMB 9,800. RMB 2,980 and RMB 4,980 are trial-only offers with strict boundaries.

## 5. Lead Scoring

Assign points:

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| company/team customer | +20 |
| monthly AI spend above RMB 5,000 | +20 |
| needs ordinary/special invoice | +20 |
| needs contract/company payment | +15 |
| purchase within 30 days | +15 |
| has technical person or existing usage | +15 |
| decision maker is in conversation | +15 |
| asks only for cheapest price | -20 |
| wants shared account/unlimited/bypass | reject |

Lead grade:

- A: 70+ points, follow within 10 minutes
- B: 40-69 points, push diagnostic/trial
- C: 10-39 points, nurture with content
- D: reject or do not spend human time

## 8. Deal Strategy

### First Deal

Default recommendation:

- if customer monthly AI spend is below RMB 5,000: sell diagnostic or trial
- if above RMB 5,000 but still cautious: sell RMB 4,980 or RMB 9,800 trial
- if above RMB 5,000 and invoice/procurement need is clear: sell RMB 29,800 Growth package
- if above RMB 20,000: sell RMB 98,000 Team package
- if production workload or multiple departments: design Enterprise package

### Anchoring

Always show three options:

| Option | Purpose |
|---|---|
| RMB 4,980 | low-risk validation |
| RMB 29,800 | recommended starter |
| RMB 98,000 | best for stable team usage |

Say:

> I do not recommend the smallest package if your team will use it every day. I also do not recommend the largest package before we see real usage. For your situation, the RMB 29,800 package is the right first step.

If the customer says the formal package is expensive:

> You do not need to start with RMB 29,800. Start with the RMB 4,980 enterprise trial. It is limited to one scenario and 14 days, but it gives your team real usage data and a report for internal approval.

### Expansion

Expansion triggers:

- 70% usage consumed
- more than 5 active internal users
- customer asks for reports/invoice/approval
- recurring weekly usage
- repeated support questions
- customer wants another department onboarded

Upgrade message:

> You have already used this as a team tool, not a one-time test. The next step is to move from trial usage to managed usage: bigger package, clearer budget cap, monthly report, and renewal plan.

## 9. Deal Rules

Never:

- start service before payment
- give unlimited quota
- accept first-time postpaid
- discount below margin floor
- sell if upstream authorization is unclear for that customer scenario
- ignore invoice entity/payment entity mismatch

Allowed concessions:

- reduce package size
- reduce support scope
- extend one diagnostic session
- include one monthly review
- add one template pack
- offer upgrade credit within 7 days

Not allowed:

- extra quota that destroys margin
- hidden price promises
- "forever price"
- "no-ban" guarantee
- personal-account workaround

## 12. Sales Metrics Dashboard

Must track:

- lead source
- lead grade
- response time
- diagnosis booking rate
- diagnosis completion rate
- quote amount
- package quoted
- invoice needed: none/ordinary/special
- payment entity
- close probability
- expected gross profit
- actual payment
- refund status
- renewal date

Weekly red flags:

- response time above 30 minutes
- quote value below 3x GMV target
- more than 20% leads are low-budget consumers
- refund rate above 3%
- support tickets above 3 per RMB 10,000 GMV
- invoice customers closed without correct invoice info
